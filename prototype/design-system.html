Design system

A studio workspace for creators who dub and cut video. It's dense, precise and calm, with one amber "tally light" accent. Monospace type is for anything you count: credits, timecodes and sizes.

Colour

Graphite surfaces in three steps. Amber is the only brand colour: primary actions, the playhead and credits. Red means record, cut or danger. Blue, violet and green are only for timeline tracks and status.

Type

Archivo for the interface, slightly condensed at display sizes. IBM Plex Mono for numbers, timecodes and labels. Noto Sans Khmer is the fallback for Khmer subtitles and UI.
