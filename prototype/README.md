# Samesay prototypes

Design source: design-system.html

Style: a dark-first studio workspace for social creators who dub and cut video, with a light theme too. Graphite surfaces in three steps and one amber "tally light" accent for primary actions, the playhead and credits. Red is for cut and danger. Fonts: Archivo for the UI, IBM Plex Mono for numbers and timecodes, and Noto Sans Khmer as the fallback. Radii are tight (4/6/10) and the layout is dense, like an editing tool.
